02 / The Statement

Whereas, We Humbly Request...

I am creating this petition to address a serious and deeply concerning issue: people who are happily in love while I am not. Honestly, how is that fair? Some people are out here enjoying their love life, going on dates, receiving sweet messages, and having someone to talk to every day, while the rest of us are just trying to survive. I believe it is time we take a stand against this clear act of emotional imbalance. As the creator of this petition, I hereby propose that all individuals who are currently in a relationship, or in a potential romantic experience, be banned. This is not necessarily because I hate seeing people happy, but because there should be limits to how much happiness one person can display in front of someone who has none. Of course, this petition is not meant to attack anyone personally (or is it?). I simply believe that a little consideration for the single population would be greatly appreciated. Is it really too much to ask for peace without being reminded that other people have romantic partners? THEREFORE, I am calling for the implementation of a strict No Lovelife Policy. No excessive couple talk, no unnecessary romantic flexing, and absolutely no making me feel like the only person who failed to unlock this particular achievement in life. By signing this petition, you agree to support this entirely serious, completely reasonable, and definitely not emotionally motivated campaign to ban people who have a love life. Thank you for your understanding. Your cooperation is greatly appreciated, especially if you are single.
